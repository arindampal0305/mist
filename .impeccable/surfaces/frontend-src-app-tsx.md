# Surface Brief: MIST Border Screening Dashboard

## Scope & Visitor Mode
- Surface: Single-Page Government Screening Dashboard (`frontend/src/App.tsx`)
- Visitor Mode: Operate (Border control officers executing 12-hour high-security screening shifts)

## Direction contract

THESIS: An administrative government intelligence dashboard that replaces "vibe-coded" neon fluff with high-density, sovereign forensic tools, cryptographic checksum grids, and Dempster-Shafer decision evidence.

OWN-WORLD: Dark Slate (`zinc-950`) base ground, Matte Zinc (`zinc-900`) card panels, fine `zinc-800` borders, Government Steel Blue (`sky-500`) primary accent, and strict risk-band state colors (emerald-500, amber-500, rose-500, red-600 pulse).

STORY: The border control officer ingests a document or triggers a scenario, inspects modular forensic evidence (ELA, MRZ checksums, facial biometrics), reads the fused Dempster-Shafer risk score and SHAP attributions, and logs a mandatory justified disposition.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 3-column operational grid (Ingestion Panel left, Technical Forensic Inspector center, Decision & Evidence Hub right) anchored by an administrative MHA/SSB header bar with live checkpoint telemetry.

FORM: Sovereign Enterprise Dashboard (Operate Mode)
